Ayu Health Suite – Cloud Clinic & EMR Platform

70% Faster Patient Triage
99.8% Prescription Accuracy
10k+ WhatsApp Alerts
The Challenge

Multi-doctor clinics struggled with fragmented paper medical records, 45-minute patient queue delays, and revenue leakage from untracked pharmacy stock.

Solution & Architecture

Architected a reactive multi-tenant EMR & billing portal with Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security), Node.js microservices, and automated WhatsApp Business API integrations for real-time patient queues and prescription delivery.

Measurable Business Impact

Reduced average patient wait times by 70%, eliminated inventory stock discrepancy to <0.2%, and automated 100% of patient follow-up reminders via WhatsApp.
